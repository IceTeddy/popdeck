# PopDeck 0.1.10

- 调整第一圈尺寸档位：小、中、大分别对应原超小、小、中，并新增更小的超小档位。
- 新增独立的中心圆直径滑块，可实时预览、自动保存并恢复默认；保留第一圈外径和图标位置。
- 同步中心圆显示、扇区内边界、点击与拖入中心删除的判定。
- 移除实验设置入口，并停用实验性圆环旋转。
- 保留已有启动项、外观偏好和 PRO 授权，继续支持应用内自动更新。

## English

- Smaller first-ring size presets, including a new Extra Small size.
- Adjust the center diameter independently with live preview, saved preferences and reset support. The first ring’s outer diameter and icon positions stay in place.
- Center rendering, sector boundaries, click handling and the center removal zone use consistent geometry.
- Experimental settings and ring rotation are unavailable.
- Existing launch items, preferences, PRO activation and in-app updates are preserved.

Minimum macOS 14; Apple Silicon. This build uses ad-hoc signing. Developer ID signing and notarization are not enabled.
